Alcoholic

Melon Ball

🔗 Link

Makes 1 drink

Source: The Kitchn

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Midori
  • 1 oz vodka
  • Fresh-squeezed orange juice, to top
  • Fresh melon balls, for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Fill a glass with ice. Add Midori and vodka.
  2. Top off with orange juice.
  3. Garnish with melon balls, if desired.

Italian

Aperitivo Numero Uno

🔗 Link

Makes 1 pitcher (scale per serving)

Source: From Chef Louisa (Villa Britannia, Taormina, Sicily)

Ingredients

Cocktail base

  • Aperol
  • Campari
  • Red vermouth (e.g. Cinzano Rosso)
  • White vermouth (e.g. Cinzano Bianco)
  • Gin

Per serving

  • A splash of fresh orange juice
  • A squeeze of lemon
  • 50ml soda water
  • Plenty of ice

To serve

  • Cherries or strawberries

Steps

  1. In a pitcher, combine equal parts Aperol, Campari, red vermouth, white vermouth, and gin with plenty of ice.
  2. Add a splash of orange juice and a squeeze of lemon. Stir.
  3. Add 50ml soda water per person. Pour into glasses over ice. Garnish with a cherry or strawberry.

Apple Pie a la Mode Shake

🔗 Link

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup frozen applesauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 cup skim milk or plain non-fat yogurt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Ice as needed

Steps

  1. Blend all ingredients until smooth. Serve immediately.

Christy's Iced Tea

🔗 Link

Makes 1 gallon

Source: From Christy Ponder

Ingredients

Tea base

  • 7 tea bags
  • 12 sprigs fresh mint
  • Rind of 3 lemons
  • 8 cups boiling water

Sweetener

  • Juice of 7 lemons
  • 2 cups sugar (or 1 cup sugar + 3 packets Sweet'n Low)

To finish

  • 8 cups cold water

Steps

  1. Steep tea bags, mint, and lemon rind in 8 cups boiling water for 12 minutes.
  2. Remove tea bags, mint, and rind. Add lemon juice and sugar; stir until dissolved.
  3. Strain into a gallon pitcher. Add 8 cups cold water.
  4. Chill and serve over ice.

Peanut Butter-Chocolate Shake

🔗 Link

Serves 1

Variations: (1) omit peanut butter, add 1/2 teaspoon peppermint flavoring; (2) add 1/2 frozen banana; (3) use vanilla instant breakfast with frozen berries, frozen pineapple, and banana or peaches.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain non-fat yogurt or skim milk
  • 1 packet chocolate instant breakfast mix
  • 2 tablespoons smooth peanut butter
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Blend all ingredients until smooth. Serve immediately.

🔗 Link

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen unsweetened berries (any combination)
  • 1 cup plain non-fat yogurt or skim milk
  • 3 tablespoons all-fruit strawberry spread (or 1 tablespoon sugar)
