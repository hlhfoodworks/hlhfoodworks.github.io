★ Lentil Chili

🔗 Link

Serves 12 (about 1 cup each)

You'll never miss the meat in this super-delicious chili -- it's a great way to serve a crowd.

Source: Low Fat & Loving It (cookbook)

Ingredients

Aromatics

  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, cut into 1/2-inch chunks
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled and coarsely chopped

Chili base

  • 7 cups water
  • 1/3 cup tomato paste
  • 2 cups (about 12 oz) brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 green pepper, cut into 1/2-inch chunks
  • 1 sweet red pepper, cut into 1/2-inch chunks

Beans and tomatoes

  • 1 (19-oz) can red kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup canned garbanzo beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cups canned whole tomatoes in puree

Seasoning

  • 1/3 cup chili powder
  • 4 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes (or more to taste)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy pot. Sauté the onion, garlic, and carrot over medium heat until the carrot is tender, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the water, tomato paste, lentils, green and red peppers, and the kidney and garbanzo beans. Add the tomatoes, breaking them up against the side of the pot. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, and Aleppo pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to moderate.
  3. Cover and cook 45 minutes, or until lentils are tender but not mushy. Add more water if necessary.
  4. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Serve alone or over plain white rice.

Smoky Chickpeas With Spinach

🔗 Link

Serves 2

Source: Marisa Moore / Marisa Moore Nutrition

Ingredients

Spice oil

  • 1 1/2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric

Chickpeas and spinach

  • 2 cups cooked chickpeas (one 15 oz can, rinsed and drained)
  • 6 cups fresh spinach
  • 2 tablespoons water or vegetable broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium. Add garlic, smoked paprika, and turmeric; stir and cook until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
  2. Add chickpeas and toss to coat in the spiced oil. Add spinach and water or broth. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until spinach has wilted.
  3. Season with salt and pepper and serve.

Chickpea Tacos

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Source: Live Eat Learn / Sarah Bond

Ingredients

Cilantro sauce

  • 1 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons mustard
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Chickpea filling

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Two 15-oz cans chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika

To serve

  • Tortillas
  • White onion, diced
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Steps

  1. Blend all cilantro sauce ingredients until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chickpeas and all spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy and golden, about 8–10 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas. Fill with crispy chickpeas, drizzle with cilantro sauce, and top with diced onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

Extra-Stuffed Veggie Burritos

🔗 Link

Serves 2

Source: Washington Post

Ingredients

Quinoa

  • 1/3 cup dried quinoa, rinsed
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice

Burrito filling

  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, seeded and thinly sliced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, seeded and thinly sliced
  • 2 scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced
  • 4 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • One 15-oz can pinto beans, undrained
  • 1 tablespoon adobo sauce from a can of chipotle chiles, or more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon granulated garlic (garlic powder)
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

Assembly

  6. Arrange mushrooms stem-side up on the pan, touching each other. Fill each with a large spoonful of marinara. Top with the sautéed spinach.
  7. Slice the goat cheese into ½-inch thick slices (roughly ½ oz each — it may crumble a bit). Press both sides of each slice into the panko topping and place on top of a mushroom. Sprinkle remaining breadcrumbs over.
  8. Bake at 425°F for 10–15 minutes until heated through, breadcrumbs are golden brown, and the goat cheese just begins to soften. Watch closely so the breadcrumbs don't burn. Serve immediately.

Truffle Mushroom Risotto

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Prep: 20 min | Cook: 20 min | Total: 40 min

A go-to "fancy" dinner that's actually easy. The risotto should flow like slow-moving lava, not form a stiff ball. Black truffle puree is available at specialty grocery stores and online.

Source: Jenny Goycochea, So Much Food

Ingredients

Risotto base

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (of 4 oz / 1 stick total, divided)
  • 2 small shallots, finely minced
  • 1½ cups arborio rice
  • ½ cup dry white wine

Broth

  • 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed

Mushrooms

  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter + 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 12 oz cremini mushrooms, quartered
  • Salt and freshly cracked pepper

Finish

  • Remaining 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons black truffle puree
  • ½ cup mascarpone
  • ⅔ cup freshly grated Parmesan, plus more for garnish
  • Salt and freshly cracked pepper

Garnish

  • Chopped chives
  • Extra Parmesan

Steps

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons butter over medium heat in a wide-bottomed skillet or pan with at least 3-inch sides. Add shallots and cook 2–3 minutes until softened. Add rice and stir to coat all grains in butter. Toast about 1 minute. Add wine and stir continuously.
  2. Once the wine is absorbed, add 1 cup warm broth, stirring constantly. Once absorbed, add another cup. Continue adding broth 1 cup at a time, stirring and waiting for each addition to absorb, until the rice is just slightly al dente. (The pan bottom exposed when you drag your spoon across it tells you it's time to add more broth.)
  3. While the rice cooks, heat 1 tablespoon butter and 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a cast iron skillet over medium-high. Add mushrooms and let sear without stirring until golden brown on one side. Stir and cook 2–3 minutes more. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. When the rice is done, turn off the heat. Stir in ⅔ of the cooked mushrooms, the truffle puree, remaining 5 tablespoons butter, mascarpone, and Parmesan. Stir until all the cheese and butter has melted. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  5. Ladle risotto into bowls. Top with the remaining mushrooms, extra Parmesan, and chopped chives.
