★ Lentil Chili

🔗 Link

Serves 12 (about 1 cup each)

You'll never miss the meat in this super-delicious chili -- it's a great way to serve a crowd.

Source: Low Fat & Loving It (cookbook)

Ingredients

Aromatics

  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, cut into 1/2-inch chunks
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled and coarsely chopped

Chili base

  • 7 cups water
  • 1/3 cup tomato paste
  • 2 cups (about 12 oz) brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 green pepper, cut into 1/2-inch chunks
  • 1 sweet red pepper, cut into 1/2-inch chunks

Beans and tomatoes

  • 1 (19-oz) can red kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup canned garbanzo beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cups canned whole tomatoes in puree

Seasoning

  • 1/3 cup chili powder
  • 4 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes (or more to taste)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy pot. Sauté the onion, garlic, and carrot over medium heat until the carrot is tender, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the water, tomato paste, lentils, green and red peppers, and the kidney and garbanzo beans. Add the tomatoes, breaking them up against the side of the pot. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, and Aleppo pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to moderate.
  3. Cover and cook 45 minutes, or until lentils are tender but not mushy. Add more water if necessary.
  4. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Serve alone or over plain white rice.

Latin/South American

Chickpea Tacos

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Source: Live Eat Learn / Sarah Bond

Ingredients

Cilantro sauce

  • 1 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons mustard
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Chickpea filling

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Two 15-oz cans chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika

To serve

  • Tortillas
  • White onion, diced
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Steps

  1. Blend all cilantro sauce ingredients until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chickpeas and all spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy and golden, about 8–10 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas. Fill with crispy chickpeas, drizzle with cilantro sauce, and top with diced onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

Sweet Potato and Black Bean Enchiladas

🔗 Link

Serves 5

The enchilada sauce calls for chicken broth; substitute vegetable broth to make fully vegetarian.

Source: Food Network / Martina McBride

Ingredients

Red enchilada sauce

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • One 15-oz can tomato sauce
  • 3/4 cup reduced-sodium chicken broth (or vegetable broth)
  • 1/2 teaspoon chipotle chile powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1–3 chipotle chiles in adobo sauce, minced (to taste)

Filling

  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 jalapeno, minced
  • 2 1/2 cups sweet potato, peeled and cubed (about 1 medium)
  • One 10-oz can diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 1 1/2 cups canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt to taste

Assembly

  • 10 whole-wheat flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend, divided

To serve

  • Sour cream

Steps

  1. Make the enchilada sauce: heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add garlic and cook 1 minute. Add tomato sauce, broth, chipotle powder, cumin, and chipotle chiles. Simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Set aside.
  2. Make the filling: heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic, onion, and jalapeno; cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add sweet potato and 2 tablespoons water; cover and cook until tender, 8–10 minutes.
  3. Stir in diced tomatoes with chiles, black beans, cilantro, cumin, and chili powder. Cook 2–3 minutes until well combined. Season with salt.
  4. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F. Spread a thin layer of enchilada sauce in a 9x13 baking dish.
  5. Fill each tortilla with a spoonful of sweet potato filling and a little cheese; roll up and place seam-side down in the dish.
  6. Pour remaining sauce over enchiladas; top with remaining cheese.
  7. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Uncover and bake 10 minutes more until cheese is melted and bubbly. Serve with sour cream.

Slow Cooker Vegan Mole Chili

🔗 Link

Serves 6 | Prep: 25 min | Cook: 5 hours | Total: ~5 hr 25 min

Rich, hearty vegan chili with mole-inspired depth. Greek yogurt is a great topping but omit to keep strictly vegan.
Tofu note: Freeze the entire tofu block until firm (overnight works — keep a block or two in the freezer as a habit). Thaw in the refrigerator, then drain as usual. Slice into 4–5 slabs and gently press out extra moisture between two plates. Finely dice per the recipe. Frozen-and-thawed tofu becomes sponge-like and soaks up the slow-cooked flavors.

Source: Emily Stoffel, The Pig & Quill

Ingredients

Dried chilies

  • 3 oz dried ancho chilies

Spice paste

  • ¼ cup light cooking oil (such as avocado)
  • 3 tablespoons chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon cacao powder
  • 1½ teaspoons Mexican oregano
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika

Chili base

  • 1 medium red onion, finely diced
  • 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 14 oz block tofu, frozen, thawed, drained, and finely diced (see note)
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 3 bay leaves
  • ¼ cup coconut sugar
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1½ oz good dark chocolate (at least 70%)
  • 3 cups mushroom stock (or vegetable stock)

To serve

  • Cilantro (optional)
  • Greek yogurt (optional; omit if strictly vegan)
  • Quinoa, rice, or other grain

Steps

  1. Remove stems and seeds from the ancho chilies. Place in a bowl, cover with very hot tap water, and steep covered for 20 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a small saucepan or skillet. Add the chili powder, cacao, oregano, cinnamon, cumin, and paprika; heat until bubbling and very fragrant, about 60 seconds. Remove from heat and set aside. Use this time to dice your veggies and prepare the tofu (see note).
  3. When the chilies have finished steeping, drain, reserving ¼ cup soaking liquid. Add chilies and reserved liquid to a food processor and blend until nearly smooth.
  4. Combine all the veggies, tofu, chickpeas, processed chilies, and spice paste in the slow cooker. Add the bay leaves, coconut sugar, salt, chocolate, and stock. Stir well. Cook on High for 5 hours, or until the vegetables have all but dissolved. Adjust salt to taste. Serve with grain and toppings of choice.

Italian

Eggplant Involtini

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Total: 1 hour

A lighter, weeknight-friendly take on eggplant parmesan — no breading, no frying. The eggplant is roasted or grilled into silky planks, rolled around a three-cheese filling, and baked in homemade tomato sauce. If you don't want to make the sauce, 3 cups of good store-bought sauce works fine.

Source: https://smittenkitchen.com/2022/09/eggplant-involtini/

Ingredients

Eggplant

  • 2 medium eggplants (about 1 pound each), tops trimmed
  • Olive oil, for brushing
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Tomato sauce

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes, to taste
  • ½ teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 (28-ounce) can whole or crushed tomatoes

Filling

  • 1 cup ricotta
  • 4 ounces mozzarella, coarsely grated
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan (of the ½ cup total)
  • Finely grated zest from half a lemon
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper

To finish

  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan (remaining)
  • Juice from half a lemon
  • Fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Prep and cook the eggplant:
    • Heat the oven to 425°F. Cut the eggplants into generous ¼-inch-thick lengthwise planks. Arrange on parchment-lined baking sheets, brush both sides with olive oil, and season well with salt and pepper.
    • To roast: Bake 10–12 minutes on the first side, flip, and roast another 8–10 minutes, until browned on each side.
    • To grill: Grill over high heat until dark marks appear, about 8 minutes on the first side and 5 on the second.
    • Set aside to cool.
  2. Make the sauce:
    • Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in an 11- to 12-inch ovenproof pot over medium. Add the garlic, salt, Aleppo pepper, and oregano; cook 1 minute.
    • Add the tomatoes (it will splatter — be careful) and stir. Simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Taste and adjust seasoning; blend if you prefer a smoother texture.
    • Ladle out 1 cup sauce and set aside for serving. If your pot isn't ovenproof, transfer the remaining sauce to a 2-quart baking dish.
  3. Make the filling: In a bowl, combine the ricotta, mozzarella, ¼ cup Parmesan, lemon zest, ½ teaspoon salt, and a few grinds of pepper.
  4. Assemble the rolls:
    • Place about 1 tablespoon of filling at the end of each eggplant plank (a little less on smaller ones), using all the filling.
    • Roll into a cigar and place seam-side down in the sauce.
  5. Bake: Brush the tops lightly with olive oil. Bake uncovered about 30 minutes, until bubbly at the edges and browned on top.
  6. Squeeze the lemon juice over the top and scatter with fresh basil. Serve with the reserved sauce and extra Parmesan on the side.

Eggplant Parmesan

🔗 Link

Serves 6–8 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 40 minutes | Total: 1 hour

Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/eggplant-parmesan/

Ingredients

Eggplant

  • 2 large eggplants, cut into ¼-inch-thick rounds

Egg wash

  • 3 large eggs, beaten

Panko coating

  • 1½ cups panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese (of the 1¼ cups total)
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
  • ½ teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling

Assembly

  • 28 ounces marinara sauce
  • 2 large fresh mozzarella balls, thinly sliced (or one package shredded mozzarella)
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese (remaining from the 1¼ cups total)
  • Extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling
  • Sea salt, for sprinkling

To serve

  • ⅓ cup fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Set up the breading stations:
    • Beat the eggs in a shallow bowl.
    • In a second shallow bowl, combine the panko, 1 cup Parmesan, oregano, thyme, Aleppo pepper, salt, and several grinds of black pepper.
  3. Bread and bake the eggplant:
    • Dip each eggplant slice in the egg wash, then press into the panko mixture to coat on all sides.
    • Arrange in a single layer on the baking sheets. Drizzle with olive oil.
    • Bake 18–25 minutes, until tender and golden brown. (Use convection setting if available for extra crispness.)
  4. Assemble and bake:
    • Spread ½ cup marinara in the bottom of a 9×13-inch baking dish.
    • Layer half the eggplant, then 1 cup marinara, then half the mozzarella. Repeat with the remaining eggplant, remaining marinara, and remaining mozzarella.
    • Top with the remaining ¼ cup Parmesan, a drizzle of olive oil, and a few pinches of sea salt.
    • Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
  5. Turn the oven to broil and broil for 2–4 minutes, until the cheese is browned in spots. Remove from the oven, top with fresh basil, and serve.

Moroccan/North African

Moroccan Eggplant with Couscous

🔗 Link

Serves 6 (serving size: 2 cups)

Source: Printed clipping

Ingredients

Spice mixture

  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper

Vegetables

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 8 cups (1/2-inch) cubed peeled eggplant (about 1 1/2 pounds)
  • 2 1/3 cups chopped tomato
  • 1 1/2 cups chopped onion
  • 1 cup chopped carrot
  • 1 cup chopped green cabbage
  • 1 tablespoon minced peeled gingerroot
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced

Liquid and couscous

  • 2 cups tomato juice
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cups uncooked couscous

Steps

  1. Combine the 5 spices in a small bowl; stir well.
  2. Heat oil in an ovenproof Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add spice mixture, then eggplant, tomato, onion, carrot, cabbage, ginger, and garlic. Sauté 3 minutes.
  3. Add tomato juice, orange juice, and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 2 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat; stir in couscous. Cover and bake at 350°F for 20 minutes.

Middle Eastern/Persian

Red Lentil Soup

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Based on Turkish red lentil soup (mercimek corbasi). Use vegetable broth for a fully vegetarian version.

Source: New York Times / Melissa Clark

Ingredients

Aromatics

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, plus more to drizzle
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon chili powder or ground cayenne

Lentils and vegetables

  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large carrot, peeled and diced

To serve

  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Olive oil drizzle
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook until golden, about 7–9 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  2. Add tomato paste, cumin, and chili powder; stir and cook 2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add stock, water, lentils, and carrot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until lentils are completely tender, about 20–25 minutes.
  4. Use an immersion blender to blend about half the soup directly in the pot (or transfer half to a blender and return). This gives a chunky-smooth texture.
  5. Stir in lemon juice; season generously with salt and pepper. Serve with a drizzle of olive oil and fresh cilantro.

Indian

Quick Chana Masala

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Ingredients

Aromatics

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Spices

  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger

Lentils

  • 1 cup (about 7 oz) red lentils, rinsed
  • One 14-oz can chopped tomatoes

Paneer (optional)

  • 1 cup paneer, cut into cubes
  • Oil for frying

To serve

  • Cooked rice or naan

  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped

  • 1 tablespoon garlic paste (or 4 cloves garlic, minced)

Tomato gravy

  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chile powder

  • One 14-oz can whole plum tomatoes, crushed by hand

  • 1 cup frozen peas, thawed

Serves 3 | Prep: 25 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 40 min

Source: Marzia, Little Spice Jar

Ingredients

Tofu and coating

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Sauce

  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1½ tablespoons grated ginger
  • 1½ tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce (or GF tamari)
  • ¼ teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons water

Stir fry

  • 3 tablespoons avocado oil
  • 2½ cups green beans, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 cup baby carrots, cut lengthwise

To serve (optional)

  • Rice, quinoa, noodles, or cauliflower rice

Steps

  1. TOFU: Drain tofu. Place on a plate with a folded tea towel, then top with another tea towel, another plate, and a heavy cast iron skillet or metal cans (or use a tofu press). Let drain 15–20 minutes or up to 1 hour. Cut into ¾–1 inch cubes and toss in 1 tablespoon cornstarch and ½ teaspoon salt in a zip-top bag until coated.

Stir the sauce to recombine, then pour it in. Stir to coat. Cook 1–2 minutes more until the tofu soaks up the sauce.
  5. Serve warm with rice, quinoa, noodles, cauliflower rice, or on its own. Best eaten fresh; the tofu loses crispness as it sits.

Baked Tofu With Peanut Sauce and Coconut-Lime Rice

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Total: ~45 min

The peanut sauce is inspired by West African groundnut stews. Fish sauce adds umami but is optional; omit it to keep the dish vegan. Peanut sauce can be made 2–3 days ahead and refrigerated.

Source: Yewande Komolafe, NYT Cooking

Ingredients

Pickled peppers

  • 4 tablespoons lime juice (of ⅔ cup total)
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 8 baby bell peppers or 1 medium bell pepper (any color), stemmed and thinly sliced lengthwise
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Coconut-lime rice

  • 1 cup long-grain rice (jasmine or basmati)
  • ½ cup full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt

Peanut sauce

  • 4 tablespoons lime juice (remaining from the ⅔ cup)
  • 1 cup smooth, natural peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon red miso
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce (optional)
  • 2 teaspoons chopped habanero pepper (stem and seeds removed) or 1 tablespoon sambal
  • 2 tablespoons peanut or vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon buckwheat honey or molasses (+ 1 tablespoon more for dressing in Step 5)
  • ¾ cup water

Tofu

  • 2 (14-oz) packages extra-firm tofu, drained and sliced crosswise ¼-inch thick
  • Peanut or vegetable oil, for brushing pan and drizzling
  • Salt

To serve

  • Zest of 1 lime
  • 3 cups peppery greens (arugula, mizuna, or baby mustard greens)
  • 2 scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 450°F and lightly brush a large rimmed sheet pan with oil.
  2. In a small bowl, stir 4 tablespoons lime juice with ½ teaspoon salt until dissolved. Add the sliced peppers and a few cracks of black pepper. Set aside to quick-pickle.
  3. In a small pot, combine the rice with 1 cup water and the coconut milk. Season with salt and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook over medium-low until just tender, 12–15 minutes. Remove from heat; keep covered until serving.
  4. In a medium bowl, whisk together the remaining 4 tablespoons lime juice, peanut butter, miso, ginger, fish sauce (if using), habanero (or sambal), 2 tablespoons oil, 1 tablespoon honey, and ¾ cup water until smooth. Season to taste with salt.
  5. Arrange tofu in a single layer on the oiled baking sheet; season with salt. Spoon about 2 tablespoons peanut sauce over each piece, coating the top and letting it run down the sides. Drizzle with a little oil and roast until the glaze is set, deep brown, and caramelized along the edges, 18–20 minutes. Add the remaining lime juice and 1 tablespoon honey to the leftover sauce in the bowl to make the dressing; set aside.
  6. Divide the greens among bowls. Stir the lime zest and half the scallions into the rice, then fluff with a fork. Top greens with rice, then the tofu. Spoon the peanut dressing over everything and garnish with the drained pickled peppers and remaining scallions.

Mushrooms Florentine

🔗 Link

Serves 8–10

Can be assembled a day ahead and refrigerated until ready to bake. To serve 4, halve all ingredients and use a greased 10-inch round or 8×8 casserole.

Source: Family recipe card, credited to Sue Karp

Ingredients

Mushrooms

  • 2 pounds small fresh mushrooms, stems removed
  • Butter, for sautéing

Spinach base

  • 4 packages (10 oz each) frozen chopped spinach, defrosted and squeezed very dry
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • Garlic salt, to taste

Cheese

  • 2 cups grated cheddar, divided

Oven Polenta with Roasted Mushrooms and Thyme

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Total: ~55 min

A "no-stir" oven polenta — the oven does the work while the mushrooms roast on the rack above. For an indulgent touch, stir a little heavy cream mixed with a finely grated garlic clove into the finished polenta.

Source: Claire Saffitz, Bon Appétit

Ingredients

Mushrooms

  • 1½ pounds mixed mushrooms (crimini, shiitake, oyster, and/or maitake), torn into 1-inch pieces
  • 4 thyme sprigs, plus thyme leaves for serving
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
  • ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil

Polenta

  • 4½ cups water
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • Kosher salt
  • 1 cup polenta

Polenta — finish

  • 4 oz Parmesan, finely grated, plus more for serving

Mushrooms — finish

  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar

To serve

  • Flaky sea salt
  • Thyme leaves
  • More Parmesan

Steps

  1. Place racks in upper and lower thirds of oven; preheat to 325°F. Combine mushrooms, thyme sprigs, and garlic on a large rimmed baking sheet. Season generously with kosher salt and pepper; drizzle with olive oil. Toss to coat, then spread in an even layer. (Don't crowd the mushrooms or they'll steam instead of crisping.) Transfer to the upper rack.
  2. Bring 4½ cups water to a simmer in a large ovenproof saucepan over medium-high heat. Add butter and a generous pinch of kosher salt; whisk to melt. Gradually whisk in the polenta (adding slowly prevents clumps). Return to a boil, immediately cover the pot, and transfer to the lower rack. Bake, shaking the mushroom pan occasionally, until polenta is tender, 25–30 minutes.
  3. Remove polenta from oven. Raise oven temperature as high as it will go (but don't broil). Continue roasting mushrooms until crispy around the edges, 5–10 more minutes.
  4. Uncover the polenta and whisk vigorously, scraping the bottom, until smooth and thick. Gradually whisk in the Parmesan until melted and incorporated. Season with kosher salt and pepper. Cover and keep warm over low heat.
  5. Remove mushrooms from oven; drizzle with red wine vinegar. Toss to coat and let cool slightly.
  6. Divide polenta among bowls and top with mushrooms, thyme leaves, flaky sea salt, and more Parmesan.

Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms with Crispy Goat Cheese

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 45 min | Total: 1 hour

Use large portobello mushrooms roughly 4 inches wide with slightly deeper caps — they're easier to stuff. Two mushrooms per serving makes a hearty main; one per person if serving as a side. For gluten-free, substitute gluten-free breadcrumbs.

Source: Laura Davidson, A Beautiful Plate

Ingredients

Marinara

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 1 (28-oz) can Italian crushed tomatoes
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Mushrooms

  • 8 large (4-inch wide) portobello mushrooms, stems trimmed
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Spinach

  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 shallot, finely sliced
  • 6 oz baby spinach
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Breadcrumb topping

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 shallot, finely diced
  • ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 garlic clove, finely minced
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Assembly

  • 4 oz goat cheese, cold from the fridge

Steps

  1. MARINARA: Warm 1 tablespoon olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the garlic and stir continuously for 30 seconds — do not let it brown. Add the tomato paste; stir over low heat for 1 minute. Add the bay leaves, oregano, and crushed tomatoes. Stir, bring to a low simmer, cover, and cook 30 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. (Can be made well ahead; reheat before using.)
  2. MUSHROOMS: Preheat oven to 450°F with rack in center. Lightly brush a sheet pan with olive oil. Place portobellos stem-side up, brush lightly with olive oil, and season with salt and pepper. Roast 15–25 minutes (check after 10 minutes), until tender. Drain any moisture that collects in the caps. Transfer to a large plate.
  3. SPINACH: Heat olive oil in a sauté pan over medium-low heat. Add the sliced shallots and sauté 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent. Add the spinach, raise heat to medium-high, and sauté until just wilted, stirring continuously. Season with salt and pepper; set aside.
  4. BREADCRUMBS: Melt the butter in a small sauté pan over medium heat. Once sizzling, add the diced shallots and a pinch of salt; cook 1–2 minutes. Add the panko and minced garlic; toast 3–4 minutes, stirring constantly, until very light golden. Transfer to a shallow bowl and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Reduce oven to 425°F. Line the same sheet pan with parchment paper (or set a baking rack inside the pan so mushrooms don't sit in released juice).
  6. Arrange mushrooms stem-side up on the pan, touching each other. Fill each with a large spoonful of marinara. Top with the sautéed spinach.
  7. Slice the goat cheese into ½-inch thick slices (roughly ½ oz each — it may crumble a bit). Press both sides of each slice into the panko topping and place on top of a mushroom. Sprinkle remaining breadcrumbs over.
  8. Bake at 425°F for 10–15 minutes until heated through, breadcrumbs are golden brown, and the goat cheese just begins to soften. Watch closely so the breadcrumbs don't burn. Serve immediately.

Truffle Mushroom Risotto

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Prep: 20 min | Cook: 20 min | Total: 40 min

A go-to "fancy" dinner that's actually easy. The risotto should flow like slow-moving lava, not form a stiff ball. Black truffle puree is available at specialty grocery stores and online.

Source: Jenny Goycochea, So Much Food

Ingredients

Risotto base

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (of 4 oz / 1 stick total, divided)
  • 2 small shallots, finely minced
  • 1½ cups arborio rice
  • ½ cup dry white wine

Broth

  • 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed

Mushrooms

  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter + 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 12 oz cremini mushrooms, quartered
  • Salt and freshly cracked pepper

Finish

  • Remaining 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons black truffle puree
  • ½ cup mascarpone
  • ⅔ cup freshly grated Parmesan, plus more for garnish
  • Salt and freshly cracked pepper

Garnish

  • Chopped chives
  • Extra Parmesan

Steps

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons butter over medium heat in a wide-bottomed skillet or pan with at least 3-inch sides. Add shallots and cook 2–3 minutes until softened. Add rice and stir to coat all grains in butter. Toast about 1 minute. Add wine and stir continuously.
  2. Once the wine is absorbed, add 1 cup warm broth, stirring constantly. Once absorbed, add another cup. Continue adding broth 1 cup at a time, stirring and waiting for each addition to absorb, until the rice is just slightly al dente. (The pan bottom exposed when you drag your spoon across it tells you it's time to add more broth.)
  3. While the rice cooks, heat 1 tablespoon butter and 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a cast iron skillet over medium-high. Add mushrooms and let sear without stirring until golden brown on one side. Stir and cook 2–3 minutes more. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. When the rice is done, turn off the heat. Stir in ⅔ of the cooked mushrooms, the truffle puree, remaining 5 tablespoons butter, mascarpone, and Parmesan. Stir until all the cheese and butter has melted. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  5. Ladle risotto into bowls. Top with the remaining mushrooms, extra Parmesan, and chopped chives.
