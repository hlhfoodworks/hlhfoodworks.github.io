★ Glazed Cinnamon Rolls (Tangzhong Version)

One dozen round rolls | Cook: 15-17 minutes

This dough uses a tangzhong (a pre-cooked flour-and-milk paste) for a softer, more tender roll and better keeping quality.
The filling here is doubled from the original for a more generous swirl, the glaze includes optional cinnamon and food coloring.

Source: The Breakfast Book, by Marion Cunningham, adapted to use a tangzhong (see https://www.theperfectloaf.com/how-to-make-tangzhong/)

Ingredients

Tangzhong (make first)

  • 1/4 cup (30g) all-purpose flour
  • 2/3 cup (160g) milk

Yeast

  • 1/4 cup warm water
  • 1 package active dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Dough

  • 3 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 pound (2 sticks) butter, chilled
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1/3 cup plus 2 tablespoons milk (the reserved 1/3 cup from the tangzhong step, plus 2 tablespoons extra)

Filling (doubled)

  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup (12 tablespoons) sugar
  • 2 teaspoons cinnamon

Glaze

  • 1 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 2 tablespoons butter, room temperature
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional)
  • Food coloring (optional, to taste)

Steps

  1. Tangzhong: In a small saucepan, whisk together the 1/4 cup (30g) flour and 2/3 cup (160g) milk until smooth. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thickened to a paste, about 2 minutes. Scrape into a small bowl; reserve 1/3 cup for the dough and discard the rest (or save for another use).
  2. Yeast: Put the warm water in a small bowl and sprinkle the yeast over. Stir in the 1 teaspoon sugar and let stand until foamy, about 5 minutes.
  3. Dry mix: In the bowl of a food processor, combine the flour, sugar, and salt; pulse briefly to blend. Add the chilled butter and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  4. Dough: Transfer the flour-butter mixture to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. Add the yeast mixture, the tangzhong, the egg yolks, and the 1/3 cup plus 2 tablespoons milk. Mix on medium speed until a smooth dough forms.
  5. Filling:
    • Combine the melted butter, sugar, and cinnamon in a small bowl to make a spreadable filling.
    • On a clean, floured countertop, roll out the whole batch of dough into one large, thin rectangle about 18-20 inches by 24 inches.
    • Spread the filling mixture evenly over the rectangle.
    • Using a pizza cutter, cut the rectangle lengthwise into 12 long strips. Roll each strip up individually into a spiral and place in a greased pan.
  6. Cover loosely and let rise for 1 hour. Meanwhile, make the glaze: Sift the confectioners' sugar into a small bowl, then stir in the butter, water, and optional cinnamon until smooth. Tint with food coloring if desired.
  7. Bake in a preheated 400°F oven for 15-17 minutes. Remove the rolls and set them on a rack over a piece of waxed paper.
  8. Spoon the prepared glaze over each roll while still hot.

★ Homemade Biscuits

8 large biscuits | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 15 to 20 minutes

Cut into 8 big squares rather than small rounds, so these come out extra generous.
Whole milk plus a splash of white vinegar stands in for buttermilk here — no need to track down real buttermilk.

Source: Adapted from Inspired Taste (inspiredtaste.net), by Adam and Joanne Gallagher

Ingredients

Soured milk (start this first)

  • 1 3/4 cups whole milk
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar

Dry ingredients

  • 4 cups (520 g) all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
  • 10 teaspoons (3 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon) aluminum-free baking powder, leveled
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons fine sea salt

Butter

  • 12 tablespoons (1 1/2 sticks; 170 g) cold butter

Steps

  1. Make the soured milk: Stir the vinegar into the whole milk. Let stand at least 5 minutes while you prepare the dough (it will look slightly curdled — that's fine).
  2. Heat the oven to 425°F. Set aside an oven-safe 10- to 12-inch skillet, preferably cast iron, or a cookie sheet lined with parchment.
  3. Dry ingredients: In a food processor, combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar, and salt. Pulse 3 to 4 times to combine.
  4. Cut the cold butter into small cubes or thin slices; scatter over the flour mixture. Pulse 5 to 7 times, just until the butter is broken into pea-sized pieces.
  5. Transfer to a large bowl. Make a well in the middle; pour in the soured milk and stir gently with a fork or spatula just until the dough comes together (some dry bits are fine — don't overmix).
  6. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured work surface. Sprinkle a little flour over the top and gently bring the dough together with your hands.
  7. Fold the dough:
    • Without overworking the dough, gently pat it into a rough rectangle about 3/4 inch thick.
    • Fold the dough into thirds, envelope-style — bring one short side over the center, then fold the opposite side over it, like a letter.
    • Rotate the dough 90 degrees, then repeat the pat-and-fold two more times to build flaky layers.
  8. Pat the dough into a rough 3/4-inch-thick rectangle. Cut into 8 squares — no need to cut rounds or reroll scraps.
  9. Arrange the squares close together on the skillet (or parchment-lined cookie sheet) to help them rise tall.
  10. Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until the tops are deeply golden brown and the biscuits have risen tall.

Raised Waffles

About 8 waffles

The batter keeps well in the refrigerator for several days.

Source: The Breakfast Book, by Marion Cunningham (originally from an early Fannie Farmer cookbook)

Ingredients

Yeast

  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 package dry yeast

Batter (mixed and left overnight)

  • 2 cups milk, warmed
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour

Added before cooking

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda

Steps

  1. Yeast: In a large mixing bowl (the batter will rise to double its volume), put the warm water and sprinkle the yeast over. Let stand until foamy, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the milk, butter, salt, sugar, and flour to the yeast mixture; beat until smooth and blended (a hand-rotary beater works well). Cover tightly and leave overnight at room temperature.
  3. Just before cooking, beat in the eggs and baking soda; stir until well mixed. The batter will be very thin.
  4. Pour about 1/2 to 3/4 cup batter into a very hot waffle iron. Bake until golden and crisp.

Baked German Pancake (or Dutch Babies)

One 12-inch pancake, or four 6-inch Dutch babies | Cook: 15-25 minutes

Source: The Breakfast Book, by Marion Cunningham

Ingredients

For the pan

  • Butter, for greasing the pan(s)

Batter

  • 3 eggs, room temperature
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted

To finish

  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • Confectioners' sugar, for dusting

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Butter one 12-inch ovenproof skillet, or four 6-inch skillets or pans with ovenproof handles.
  2. Beat the eggs in a mixing bowl until thoroughly combined, then add the milk and blend well.
  3. Sift the flour and salt over the egg mixture, whisking steadily until smooth. Add the melted butter and mix briskly until combined.
  4. Pour the batter into the pan(s). Bake for 15 minutes at 450°F for small pancakes; for one large pancake, bake 15 minutes at 450°F then reduce to 350°F and bake 10 minutes more.
  5. Sprinkle the lemon juice over the pancake(s) and dust with confectioners' sugar. Serve at once, while puffed and hot.

Baked Crunchy Hot Honey Chicken

6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes | Total: 40 minutes

Source: Half Baked Harvest, by Tieghan Gerard

Ingredients

Crumb coating

  • 6 cups cornflakes (gluten-free if needed)
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

Egg wash

  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 tablespoons hot sauce

Chicken

  • 2 pounds chicken breast tenderloins
  • Extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling

Hot honey sauce

  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons hot sauce
  • 1 to 3 teaspoons cayenne pepper
  • 3/4 teaspoon chipotle chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Sea salt

To finish

  • Fresh thyme, cilantro, or parsley

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Pulse the cornflakes, parmesan, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt in a food processor until fine crumbs form. Transfer to a shallow bowl.
  3. Beat the eggs with the hot sauce in a bowl; add the chicken and toss well to coat.
  4. Dredge the chicken through the crumbs, covering fully. Place on the baking sheet. For a thicker coating, dip back through the egg wash and crumbs a second time.
  5. Drizzle the chicken with olive oil. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until crisp all around.
  6. Just before the chicken is done, warm the honey, hot sauce, cayenne, chili powder, onion powder, and garlic powder with a pinch of salt in a small saucepan over low heat until combined.
  7. Drizzle the warm sauce over the chicken. Top with fresh herbs.

Brown Butter Sage Skillet Chicken

4 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes | Total: 30 minutes

Thin-sliced chicken breasts cook fastest; if starting with standard breasts, slice horizontally through the middle for thinner cutlets.
Great served over egg noodles, orzo, or a light pasta.

Source: Nourish and Fete, by Monica

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced or pounded thin
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour

Sear

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (of the 6 tablespoons below)
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil

Brown butter sauce

  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (the remainder of the 6 tablespoons total)
  • 1/3 cup fresh sage leaves
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon (reserve the other half for serving)
  • 1/2 cup white wine or low-sodium chicken broth

To serve

  • Lemon wedges

Steps

  1. Season both sides of the chicken generously with salt and pepper. Place the flour in a shallow bowl or plate and dredge each cutlet, shaking off the excess.
  2. Add 2 tablespoons of the butter and the olive oil to a large skillet over medium-high heat. When the butter melts, add the chicken and cook until golden brown, about 3 to 4 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Add the remaining 4 tablespoons butter to the skillet. When melted, add the sage leaves. Let the butter brown and bubble until it smells nutty and the sage is crispy, about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the lemon juice and wine (or broth) to the skillet. Simmer 2 to 3 minutes, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Return the chicken to the skillet and warm through. Serve with lemon wedges.

★ Company Baked Chicken

Marked "v. good!" on the original card.

Source: Family recipe card, credited to Grandmother Brenda

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 chickens or equivalent amount of chicken parts
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder

Marinade

  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons oil

To finish

  • Sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Season the chicken pieces all over with pepper and garlic powder.
  2. Combine the honey, soy sauce, and oil to make the marinade.
  3. Add the chicken to the marinade, turning to coat. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator 24 hours, turning frequently.
  4. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  5. Place the chicken skin-side down in a baking dish with the marinade. Bake uncovered 45 minutes.
  6. Turn the chicken pieces over, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and continue baking another 30 minutes, basting occasionally with the pan juices.

Creamy Spinach-Artichoke Chicken Stew

4 to 6 | Total: 50 minutes

Source: NYT Cooking, by Sarah DiGregorio

Ingredients

Aromatics

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 large yellow or red onion, finely chopped
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 3 celery stalks, chopped
  • 8 garlic cloves, smashed and chopped

Braise

  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 3/4 cup white wine
  • 2 to 2 1/4 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced (about 1 1/2 tablespoons)
  • 1 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes

Spinach and artichokes

  • 1 (10-ounce) package frozen cut spinach
  • 1 (12-ounce) jar marinated artichoke hearts, drained (about 1 heaping cup)

To finish

  • 1/2 cup cream cheese (about 4 ounces)
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped fresh dill
  • 4 to 6 scallions, thinly sliced
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Aromatics:
    • In a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat, melt the butter. Add the onion, season lightly with salt, and cook, stirring, until softened, about 5 minutes.
    • Add the celery and cook, stirring, until softened, about 5 minutes, adjusting the heat as needed to avoid scorching. Stir in the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  2. Pour in the stock and wine, and bring to a bubble. Add the chicken thighs, lemon juice, and Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes.
  3. Reduce the heat to maintain a low simmer and simmer, uncovered, 20 minutes.
  4. Add the frozen spinach and artichoke hearts. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook, stirring, to help the spinach defrost and the liquid reduce slightly, about 5 minutes.
  5. Add the cream cheese in dollops, stirring to melt it into the stew. Add the dill. Using two forks, coarsely break or shred the chicken into pieces. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  6. Divide among bowls and top with scallions and Parmesan.

Crispy Chicken With Lime Butter

4 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 35 minutes | Total: 40 minutes

The rendered chicken fat (schmaltz) can be saved to pan-fry vegetables, enrich a soup or sauce, or spread on toast.

Source: NYT Cooking, by Eric Kim

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4 large bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 pounds)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon peanut or canola oil

Aromatics

  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed

Pan sauce

  • 1/2 cup chicken stock or 1/4 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, plus wedges for serving
  • 2 teaspoons maple syrup
  • 3 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, cut into pats

To serve

  • Parsley, cilantro, basil, or mint leaves (optional)

Steps

  1. Pat the chicken dry and season with salt and pepper. If you have time, let sit at room temperature 10 to 30 minutes.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the oil and swirl to coat the pan. Place the chicken skin side down and cook undisturbed until the skin is deeply golden and crispy, about 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic to the pan. Flip the chicken and cook until the bottom is lightly browned and the meat is cooked through, about 5 to 8 minutes more. Transfer to a plate.
  4. Pan sauce: Add the chicken stock, lime juice, and maple syrup to the skillet. Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook until slightly reduced, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat and whisk in the cold butter a pat at a time until the sauce is glossy.
  5. Serve the chicken with the pan sauce, lime wedges, and the optional fresh herbs (lightly spritzed with lime juice and seasoned with flaky salt, if using).

★ Crispy Spice Rubbed Chicken Thighs

3 to 4 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes | Total: 35 minutes

Source: Fifteen Spatulas

Ingredients

Spice rub

  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon ground chipotle pepper
  • 2 teaspoons yellow curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sea salt

Chicken

  • 2 to 3 pounds skin-on, bone-in chicken thighs (about 5 pieces)
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Combine the ginger, chipotle pepper, curry powder, paprika, garlic powder, coriander, cardamom, cloves, cayenne, cumin, and salt in a small bowl.
  3. Trim excess fat from the chicken thighs. Rub the thighs all over with the spice mixture.
  4. Heat an ovenproof skillet over medium heat with enough olive oil to coat the bottom (about 2 tablespoons). Cook the thighs skin-side down, undisturbed, until the skin is deeply golden brown and crispy, about 10 minutes.
  5. Flip and cook 5 more minutes, then transfer the skillet to the oven. Bake 10 to 15 minutes more, until cooked through (internal temperature 165°F).

Grilled Buffalo Wings

6 to 8 | Total: 35 minutes

Crowding the wings close together on the grill is intentional — it creates steam that helps render the fat and keep the meat moist before crisping.

Source: Food Network, by Katie Lee Biegel, from The Kitchen

Ingredients

Dry rub

  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder

Wings

  • 3 pounds whole chicken wings

Buffalo sauce

  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup hot sauce
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Steps

  1. Dry rub: In a small bowl, combine the salt, pepper, and garlic powder. In a large bowl, toss the wings with the rub to coat evenly.
  2. Preheat a gas grill to medium heat (about 350°F).
  3. Place the wings on the grill, crowding them close together so they're all touching. Grill, covered, 20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the butter, hot sauce, vinegar, and honey in a saucepan over low heat, whisking to combine.
  5. In a clean large bowl, toss the wings with the sauce. Turn the grill up to medium-high. Use tongs to return the wings to the grill and cook, uncovered, turning once, until charred in spots, about 5 minutes more.

Skillet Chicken and Zucchini With Charred Scallion Salsa

4 | Total: 40 minutes

Any seasonal, quick-roasting vegetable can stand in for the zucchini — cherry tomatoes or asparagus both work well.
If cilantro isn't your thing, basil is a good substitute, or use a mix of the two.

Source: NYT Cooking, by Colu Henry

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4 medium bone-in, skin-on chicken breasts (about 3 to 3 1/2 pounds), at room temperature
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons canola or grapeseed oil

Zucchini

  • 1 1/2 pounds zucchini (3 to 4 medium), quartered lengthwise, then halved crosswise

Salsa

  • 1/4 cup fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 1 medium jalapeño, thinly sliced, seeded if you prefer
  • 1 bunch scallions (about 7 or 8), trimmed and halved crosswise
  • 1 cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon light brown sugar
  • Flaky sea salt (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Pat the chicken very dry and season generously all over with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a 12-inch cast-iron pan or other large, ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat. Once shimmering, add the chicken skin-side down and cook undisturbed until the skin is deeply golden, about 5 minutes. Transfer skin-side up to a plate.
  3. Remove the pan from the heat. Add the zucchini and toss to coat in the chicken fat; season with salt and pepper and arrange in an even layer. Nestle the chicken, skin-side up, on top of the zucchini. Roast until the chicken is cooked through, 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. Salsa:
    • In a medium bowl, stir together the lime juice and jalapeño; set aside.
    • Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the scallions and cook, tossing frequently, until they begin to brown and char slightly, about 5 minutes.
    • Finely chop the scallions and toss with the lime mixture.
    • Stir in the cilantro, olive oil, and brown sugar until the sugar dissolves. Season to taste with salt.
  5. Remove the chicken from the oven and let rest on a plate or cutting board about 5 minutes (return the zucchini to the oven to keep warm). Spoon the salsa over the chicken and zucchini.

Spring Chicken Paillard

4 | Total: 1 hour 25 minutes (includes marinating)

Inspired by the chicken paillard at Pastis in New York City.

Source: Food Network, by Katie Lee Biegel

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4 small/medium boneless, skinless chicken breasts

Marinade

  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons chopped fresh rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Green olive tapenade

  • 1 cup fresh parsley leaves
  • 1/2 cup pitted Castelvetrano olives
  • 2 tablespoons thinly sliced fresh chives, plus more for garnish
  • 1 teaspoon capers, drained
  • 1/2 teaspoon honey
  • 2 anchovies
  • 1 small shallot, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 lemon, zested and juiced
  • Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper

Sautéed greens

  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 (8-ounce) bunch Swiss chard, stems trimmed, roughly chopped
  • 1 (8-ounce) bunch lacinato kale, stems removed, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar

To serve

  • Lemon wedges

Steps

  1. Chicken: Place a chicken breast on a cutting board, cover with plastic wrap, and pound with a meat mallet or rolling pin to an even 1/4-inch thickness. Repeat with remaining breasts.
  2. Whisk together the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, rosemary, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, thyme, oregano, and pepper in a shallow dish. Add the chicken and turn to coat.
  3. Cover and marinate in the fridge at least 30 minutes, up to 3 hours.
  4. Green olive tapenade: Meanwhile, in a food processor, combine the parsley, olives, chives, capers, honey, anchovies, shallot, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Pulse until finely chopped but not pureed. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Let the chicken sit at room temperature while you preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high heat.
  6. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through (165°F), 3 to 4 minutes per side.
  7. Remove to a platter and immediately spread the tapenade over the chicken so it soaks in the flavors.
  8. Sautéed greens: Heat the olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium heat until shimmering. Add the Swiss chard and kale and season with salt and pepper.
  9. Cook just until wilted (still some body), 3 to 4 minutes.
  10. Remove from the heat and drizzle with the red wine vinegar. Serve warm on top of the chicken. Garnish with more chives and lemon wedges.

Weeknight Fancy Chicken and Rice

4 to 6 | Total: 50 minutes

Garnish is flexible — dried cranberries, hazelnuts, or pine nuts can stand in for or join the apricots and almonds.

Source: NYT Cooking, from Asha Gomez and Martha Hall Foose, adapted by Sara Bonisteel

Ingredients

  • 1 small onion, minced
  • 1 small carrot, minced

  • 2 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh minced Italian parsley
  • 1 heaped teaspoon minced fresh rosemary, or 1/2 teaspoon crumbled dried rosemary
  • 1/4 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes

Mushrooms and wine

  • 1/2 pound mushrooms, trimmed and sliced

Tomatoes

  • 1 28-ounce can chopped tomatoes, pulsed in a food processor

    • Drain through a strainer lined with cheesecloth or a paper towel, set over a bowl, reserving the liquid.
    • Rinse the mushrooms in several changes of water, squeeze out excess water, and chop coarsely.

  • 2 (7 to 8 ounce) chicken breasts, skinless and boneless

  • 1/4 cup flour

Frying

  • Oil for frying (canola or vegetable, about 4 cups)

  • Parsley, finely chopped

  • 2 Tbsp whole pink or black peppercorns

  • 2 Tbsp onion powder
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 Tbsp light brown sugar

  • 1 (12-14 lb.) turkey, neck and giblets removed

Glaze

  • Small handful hardy herbs (sage, rosemary, bay leaves, and/or thyme)
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 (2x1") strips orange zest

★ Horseradish Sauce

Serves 8 | Total: 5 min

A classic accompaniment to Four Peppercorn Crusted Rotisserie Rib Roast. Note: prepared horseradish in the jar already contains vinegar — you may want to reduce or omit the added cider vinegar. Can also be made with nonfat sour cream and low-fat mayo.

Source: CAROL46, Allrecipes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 3 Tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 Tbsp prepared horseradish
  • 1 Tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1/8 tsp mustard powder
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Whisk all ingredients together in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

★ Cherry Barbecue Sauce

Makes about 3½ cups | Total: 30 min

Source: Ilene Harrington, Taste of Home

Ingredients

Sauté

  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced

Sauce

  • 2 cups fresh or frozen dark sweet cherries, pitted and coarsely chopped
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 2/3 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp ground mustard
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/8 tsp Liquid Smoke (optional)

Steps

  1. In a large saucepan, sauté onion in butter until tender, about 5 minutes. Add garlic; cook 1 minute longer.
  2. Stir in remaining ingredients. Cook uncovered over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until cherries are tender and sauce has thickened, about 20 minutes.

Authentic Chimichurri

Serves 8 | Total: 10 min (ideally rests 2 hrs)

Source: Karina Carrel, Cafe Delites

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, finely chopped or minced
  • 2 small red chilies (or 1), deseeded and finely chopped (about 1 Tbsp)
  • 3/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp coarse salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper

Steps

  1. Finely chop garlic and chili.
  2. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl until well combined.
  3. Let sit at least 5–10 minutes before serving (ideally 2 hours for fuller flavor). Use to baste meats while grilling, or serve as a condiment.
