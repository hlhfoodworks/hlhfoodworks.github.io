Nechamie's Challah

🔗 Link

Makes 2 large loaves

Source: From Nechamie (Chabad Jewish Center of Pudong)

🏔 High altitude version

Ingredients

Yeast starter

  • 1/4 cup hot water
  • 2 packages yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Dough

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water
  • 1/2 cup oil (slightly less)
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 7 cups flour

Glaze

  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Sesame seeds or poppy seeds

Yeast starter

  • 1/4 cup hot water
  • 1 1/2 packages yeast (3 1/4 teaspoons)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Dough

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water
  • 1/2 cup oil (slightly less)
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 7 cups flour

Glaze

  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Sesame seeds or poppy seeds

Steps

  1. Dissolve yeast in 1/4 cup hot water with 1 teaspoon sugar; let stand 2 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add 1 1/2 cups warm water, oil, 3/4 cup sugar, salt, and eggs to the yeast mixture; stir to combine.
  3. Gradually add flour 2 cups at a time; stir until the dough becomes stiff. Turn out and knead for 7-8 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
  5. Punch down, divide into 2 portions, and shape into braided loaves. Place on greased baking sheets.
  6. Let rise again until doubled, about 30 minutes.
  7. Brush with glaze (egg beaten with sugar); sprinkle with sesame or poppy seeds.
  8. Bake at 375° for 30-45 minutes until deep golden brown. Cool on racks.

Eric's Chocolate Chip Cookies

🔗 Link

About 3 dozen cookies

Based on the classic Toll House chocolate chip cookie recipe, with a few of Eric's own touches — rolled oats and roughly chopped pecans.

Based on the classic Toll House chocolate chip cookie recipe, with a few of Eric’s own touches — rolled oats and roughly chopped pecans.

Source: Base recipe: Nestlé Toll House (https://www.verybestbaking.com/toll-house/recipes/chocolate-chip-cookies/)

🏔 High altitude version

Ingredients

Dry ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup rolled quick oats
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Creamed base

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar

Added to the creamed base

  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs

Folded in

  • 2 cups (12 ounces) semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup pecans, very roughly chopped into big pieces

Dry ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups + 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup rolled quick oats
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Creamed base

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 11 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 11 tablespoons packed brown sugar

Added to the creamed base

  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs

Folded in

  • 2 cups (12 ounces) semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup pecans, very roughly chopped into big pieces

Steps

  1. Whisk together the flour, oats, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl; set aside.
  2. Cream the butter and sugars: In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar on medium-high speed until pale and fluffy, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Add the vanilla and eggs; continue beating on medium speed until fully incorporated and the mixture looks well emulsified.
  4. With the mixer on low speed (or by hand), add the flour mixture and mix just until no dry streaks remain — the dough should be stiff.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips and pecans by hand.
  6. Cover and refrigerate the dough for at least 1 hour (and up to 2 days) to firm up before baking.
  7. When ready to bake, preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line baking sheets with parchment.
  8. Using an ice cream scoop, portion generous mounds of dough onto the prepared sheets, spacing them a few inches apart.
  9. Bake 11-13 minutes, until the edges are golden brown and the centers still look slightly underbaked. Cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring.

Ingredients

Wet

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup sugar

Dry

  • 2 cups flour

Cream cheese layer

  • 4 tablespoons butter, softened
  • 6 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar

  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Brownie batter

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar

  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup flour

Chocolate base

  • 8 oz baking chocolate

Cream cheese layer

  • 4 tablespoons butter, softened
  • 6 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons flour

  • 4 eggs

  • 1 cup + 2 tablespoons flour

  6. Pour cream cheese layer evenly over brownie batter.
  7. Drop reserved brownie batter by spoonfuls over cream cheese layer and swirl gently with a knife.
  8. Bake at 350° for 35-40 minutes until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs.

Nut Butter Balls

🔗 Link

About 4 dozen | Bake: 325°F for 25 minutes

Card notes 'double recipe' — these quantities are the base (single) recipe.

Source: Family recipe card (Grandmother Brenda's collection)

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 pound (2 sticks) butter, softened
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla

Coating

  • 1 cup chopped nuts

Finish

  • Powdered sugar, for rolling

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
  2. Sift together flour, sugar, and salt. Add softened butter and vanilla; work together with hands until a dough forms.
  3. Shape into 1-inch balls, then roll each ball in the chopped nuts to coat.
  4. Place on an ungreased baking sheet. Bake 25 minutes.
  5. While still warm, roll in powdered sugar to coat. Let cool completely.

Apple Pie

🔗 Link

Makes 1 pie

Source: From Susan Muhlheim

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup shortening
  • 3-4 tablespoons water

Filling

  • 5 apples of at least 3 different varieties, peeled and sliced into 8 pieces each
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar (divided)
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoons margarine or butter

Steps

  1. Crust: Stir together flour and salt. Cut in shortening until pieces are the size of peas. Add water gradually, tossing with fork, until all is moistened. Roll out pastry to approximately 12" diameter on floured surface. Mold edges to pie plate.
  2. Toss sliced apples with 1/4 cup of the brown sugar. Arrange in pie crust.
  3. Combine remaining brown sugar and flour; cut in margarine until crumbly. Sprinkle mixture over apples.
  4. Bake at 375 for 40 minutes.

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the water and yeast. Add the flour and salt and mix with one hand until it just forms a smooth dough. Cover and let rise at room temperature for 12–24 hours. It will nearly double in size.
  2. After this initial ferment, place the dough in the fridge for another 12–48 hours.

Susan's Calzones

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Source: Family recipe card, credited to Susan

Ingredients

Dough (5 hours ahead)

  • 12 Rhodes frozen dinner rolls (3 per person), defrosted and risen

Filling

  • 1 pound ground beef or ground turkey
  • About 1/2 cup spaghetti sauce, or enough to hold filling together

Cheese

  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan

Add-ins (optional)

  • Sliced olives
  • Pepperoni
  • Sautéed mushrooms

Topping (optional)

  • Additional spaghetti sauce
  • Additional grated parmesan

Steps

  1. Defrost rolls at room temperature and let rise until doubled, about 5 hours (or per package directions).
  2. Brown ground beef or turkey in a skillet over medium heat; drain fat.
  3. Mix browned meat with spaghetti sauce (enough to hold the mixture together but not soupy), mozzarella, and parmesan. Stir in any optional add-ins.
  4. On a lightly floured surface, press each group of 3 risen rolls together and roll into a rough square.
  5. Spoon filling onto one half of each square. Fold dough over to form a triangle. Press edges firmly with fork tines to seal.
  6. Place on a baking sheet. Bake at 350°F for 18 minutes.
  7. Optional: 10 minutes in, spoon additional spaghetti sauce over the top and sprinkle with parmesan; return to oven for remaining 8 minutes.
