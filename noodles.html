Creamy Baked Mac and Cheese

🔗 Link

Serves 8-10

Always shred cheese from the block -- pre-shredded bags have anti-caking agents that inhibit melting.
For a crunchy topping, mix 1 cup panko with 2 tablespoons melted butter (or 1 cup crushed Ritz crackers with 2 tablespoons butter) and sprinkle on top before baking.
Can be assembled ahead, refrigerated 1-2 days, and baked as directed, adding 25-35 minutes covered.
Total: 35 min active; 15-35 min bake.

Source: thechunkychef.com

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 1 pound dried elbow pasta
  • Salt

Cheese sauce

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 2 1/2 cups half-and-half
  • 3 cups mixed shredded cheddar and Gruyere (for sauce -- see Comments for totals)
  • 1/2 tablespoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika

Cheese layers

  • 3 cups mixed shredded cheddar and Gruyere (1 1/2 cups for inner layer, 1 1/2 cups for topping)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Cook pasta in salted water 1 minute less than package directions for al dente. Drain.
  3. Shred all cheese from the block and toss together (total: 4 cups cheddar + 2 cups Gruyere). Divide into three portions: 3 cups for the sauce, 1 1/2 cups for the inner layer, 1 1/2 cups for the topping.
  4. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in flour (it will look like wet sand); cook 1 minute. Slowly whisk in half-and-half, then milk, whisking constantly until smooth. Heat over medium, whisking frequently, until thickened to a semi-condensed-soup consistency.
  5. Remove from heat. Stir in salt, pepper, paprika, and the 3-cup sauce portion of cheese in two additions, stirring each until completely melted.
  6. Combine drained pasta with cheese sauce. Pour half into the baking dish. Sprinkle with inner-layer cheese, then add remaining pasta. Sprinkle topping cheese on top.
  7. Bake 15 minutes until bubbly and lightly golden. For a crunchier, firmer result, bake at 350-375 degrees F for 20-30 minutes, then broil 2-5 minutes.

Four-Cheese Truffled Macaroni and Cheese

🔗 Link

Serves 12 | Total: 1 hr 15 min

Truffle salt can also be sprinkled over the top before serving. One small bottle of truffle oil goes a very long way.
Shred cheeses from the block for best melt.

Source: Allrecipes (Hilary van Santen)

Ingredients

Pasta

  • Salt
  • 1 (16-oz) package cavatappi (corkscrew macaroni)

Cheese sauce

  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 5 1/2 cups milk, divided (2 cups first, then remaining 3 1/2 cups)
  • 2 1/2 cups shredded smoked Gouda
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Swiss cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan (from 1 1/2 cups total)

Topping

  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan (remaining)
  • 1/2 cup dry bread crumbs
  • 1 tablespoon truffle oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Cook cavatappi in salted boiling water until al dente, about 8 minutes. Drain.
  3. While pasta cooks, melt butter in a large pot over medium-low. Whisk in flour to a thick paste, about 30 seconds. Slowly whisk in 2 cups milk until smooth, then stir in remaining 3 1/2 cups milk. Increase heat to medium-high and cook, whisking constantly, until thick and almost boiling, 5-10 minutes.
  4. Add Gouda, cheddar, Swiss, and 1 cup Parmesan; stir until melted and smooth. Fold in cavatappi. Pour into prepared baking dish.
  5. Combine remaining 1/2 cup Parmesan with bread crumbs; sprinkle over pasta. Drizzle truffle oil on top.
  6. Bake until bubbling and topping is golden, about 30 minutes. Cool 10 minutes before serving.

Italian

Browned Garlic Butter Creamed Corn Ravioli

🔗 Link

Serves 6 | Prep: 10 min | Total: 25 min

Use heavy cream for richest result; canned coconut milk or whole milk work as lighter options.

Source: Half Baked Harvest (halfbakedharvest.com)

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 1 pound cheese ravioli, homemade or store-bought

Sauce base

  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 medium shallot, chopped
  • 3-4 ears corn, kernels removed from the cob
  • 4 tablespoons salted butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme, chopped, plus more for serving
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes

Sauce

  • 1/2 cup dry white wine (such as Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream, canned coconut milk, or whole milk
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan or Asiago cheese
  • 1/3 cup fresh basil, plus more for serving

To serve

  • Reserved corn and butter (from above)
  • Fresh basil and fresh thyme

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook ravioli according to package directions until al dente. Drain.
  2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. When the oil shimmers, add shallot and corn. Cook 5 minutes until corn is golden. Add butter, garlic, and thyme; season with salt and pepper. Cook until butter begins to brown and corn begins to crisp, 3-4 minutes. Season with Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes. Remove 1-2 large spoonfuls of corn and butter from the skillet and set aside for serving.
  3. Reduce heat to low. Pour in wine and simmer 2 minutes. Add cream and Parmesan; cook a few more minutes until warmed through. Add drained ravioli and toss gently to combine. Remove from heat and fold in basil.
  4. Divide among plates and top with reserved corn, fresh basil, and thyme.

Classic Stuffed Shells

🔗 Link

Serves 6 | Total: 55 min

Can be assembled up to 8 hours ahead, covered, and refrigerated; add 10 extra minutes to baking time.
Freezes well uncooked (up to 3 months); thaw overnight and bake as directed.
Use whole-milk ricotta and mozzarella for best flavor. Shred your own cheese for best melt.

Source: Sara Welch, dinneratthezoo.com

Ingredients

Shells

  • 24 jumbo pasta shells
  • Cooking spray

Filling

  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella (from 3 cups total)
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan

Assembly

  • 3 cups marinara sauce, divided (1 1/2 cups for base; 1 1/2 cups for topping)
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella (remaining)

To serve

  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Coat a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray. Spread 1 1/2 cups marinara sauce in the bottom.
  2. Cook shells in salted boiling water until just shy of al dente (1-2 minutes less than package directions; they will finish in the oven). Drain.
  3. Combine ricotta, 1 1/2 cups mozzarella, Italian seasoning, egg, salt, pepper, and Parmesan. Mix well.
  4. Fill each shell with the ricotta mixture and arrange in the baking dish. Spoon remaining 1 1/2 cups marinara over shells, then sprinkle remaining 1 1/2 cups mozzarella on top.
  5. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Uncover and bake 10 more minutes until cheese is melted and starting to brown. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

Crispy-Crackly Minty-Pea Lasagna

🔗 Link

Serves 8

Meyer lemon is optional but adds a bright floral note when layered in. No-boil noodles are not recommended -- use standard dry lasagna noodles.
The cracker-crisp top is the signature of this recipe; bake until noodles are very burnished. Everyone gets a crunchy piece.
Active: 30 min; Bake: 60-65 min.

Source: Molly Baz, "The Club"

Ingredients

Noodles

  • 1 pound dry lasagna noodles
  • Kosher salt
  • Extra-virgin olive oil (for greasing noodles and pan)

Filling

  • 1 (16-oz) bag frozen peas, defrosted (10 oz / about 2 1/2 cups for filling; 6 oz reserved for assembly)
  • 1 (10-oz) bag frozen spinach, defrosted and squeezed very dry
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 1 pound fresh ricotta
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2/3 cup heavy cream
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 3/4 oz Pecorino Romano, finely grated (half of 3 1/2 oz total)
  • 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 cups packed fresh mint leaves

Mozzarella layer

  • 8 oz whole milk mozzarella (not fresh), grated on the large holes of a box grater

Assembly (optional)

  • 1 Meyer lemon, roughly chopped (seeds discarded)

Crispy top

  • Extra-virgin olive oil (for drizzling)
  • 1 3/4 oz Pecorino Romano, finely grated (remaining half)

Steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles in a large pot of generously salted boiling water 2-3 minutes less than package directions (very al dente). Drain and transfer to a sheet pan; coat lightly with olive oil.

A highly adaptable weeknight dish in the tradition of wafu (Japanese-style pasta).
Umami variations: swap soy sauce for Parmesan, miso, seaweed, or mushrooms.
Heat variations: swap black pepper for ginger or Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes.
Protein additions: boil eggs or shelled edamame in the pasta water before the udon, or add tinned mackerel or fresh yuba with the sesame seeds.
If udon are unavailable, use the dried thinner variety resembling linguine.

Source: New York Times (Ali Slagle)

Ingredients

Noodles

  • Salted water
  • 14-16 oz udon noodles (preferably thick fresh, frozen, or shelf-stable)
  • 1 pound baby spinach, or mature spinach coarsely chopped

Brown butter sauce

  • 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper, plus more for serving
  • 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce, plus more as needed
  • Pinch of granulated sugar

To finish

  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds, plus more for serving

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook udon per package directions until just tender. Reserve 1 cup cooking water. Add spinach and press to submerge; it will continue cooking later. Drain noodles and spinach, shaking off excess water.
  2. Set the same pot over medium heat. Add 5 tablespoons butter and cook, stirring occasionally, until foam subsides and milk solids turn golden-brown and smell nutty, 3-4 minutes. Add black pepper; stir until fragrant. Add 1/4 cup pasta water, then noodles and spinach, soy sauce, and sugar. Toss until sauce thickens and clings to the noodles. Add pasta water 1 tablespoon at a time as needed to keep sauce silky.
  3. Remove from heat. Add sesame seeds and stir in remaining 1 tablespoon butter until melted. Season with more soy sauce and pepper if mild, or a pinch more sugar if too salty. Serve with extra sesame seeds on top.
