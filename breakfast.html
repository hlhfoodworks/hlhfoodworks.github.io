Baked German Pancake (or Dutch Babies)

🔗 Link

One 12-inch pancake, or four 6-inch Dutch babies | Cook: 15-25 minutes

Source: The Breakfast Book, by Marion Cunningham

Ingredients

For the pan

  • Butter, for greasing the pan(s)

Batter

  • 3 eggs, room temperature
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted

To finish

  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • Confectioners' sugar, for dusting

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Butter one 12-inch ovenproof skillet, or four 6-inch skillets or pans with ovenproof handles.
  2. Beat the eggs in a mixing bowl until thoroughly combined, then add the milk and blend well.
  3. Sift the flour and salt over the egg mixture, whisking steadily until smooth. Add the melted butter and mix briskly until combined.
  4. Pour the batter into the pan(s). Bake for 15 minutes at 450°F for small pancakes; for one large pancake, bake 15 minutes at 450°F then reduce to 350°F and bake 10 minutes more.
  5. Sprinkle the lemon juice over the pancake(s) and dust with confectioners' sugar. Serve at once, while puffed and hot.

Baked Stuffed French Toast

🔗 Link

Serves 6

Source: From Susan Muhlheim

Ingredients

Bread base

  • 4 large slices sourdough bread, crusts removed and cubed

Filling

  • 8 oz cream cheese, cubed
  • 1 large Granny Smith apple, peeled and chopped

Custard

  • 6 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon

To finish

  • Powdered sugar

Steps

  1. Layer half the bread cubes in an ungreased 11x7 baking dish. Distribute cream cheese and apple over the bread, then top with remaining bread cubes.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, and cinnamon together; pour evenly over the bread.
  3. Bake at 375° for 35 minutes until puffed and golden.
  4. Sprinkle with powdered sugar before serving.

Best Blueberry Muffins (More Berries Than Batter)

🔗 Link

Makes 12 muffins

Keeps wrapped individually and frozen up to 1 month; thaw overnight, rewarm in 350°F oven 10 minutes.
Variations: lemon-blueberry (add 1 lemon's zest to dry ingredients); whole-wheat (replace 3/4 cup flour with whole-wheat); blackberry or raspberry (substitute 2 cups halved blackberries or whole raspberries).

Keeps wrapped individually and frozen up to 1 month; thaw overnight, rewarm in 350°F oven 10 minutes.
Variations: lemon-blueberry (add 1 lemon's zest to dry ingredients); whole-wheat (replace 3/4 cup flour with whole-wheat); blackberry or raspberry (substitute 2 cups halved blackberries or whole raspberries).

Source: Los Angeles Times

🏔 High altitude version

Ingredients

Dry ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons granulated sugar (reserve 1 tablespoon for topping)
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Berries

  • 2 slightly heaping cups (12 oz) fresh or frozen blueberries

Wet ingredients

  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon almond extract (optional)
  • 1 large egg

Topping

  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar (reserved from above)
  • Cold butter, for serving

Dry ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups + 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar (reduced from 1/2 cup + 2 tablespoons; keep 1 tablespoon for topping)
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Berries

  • 2 slightly heaping cups (12 oz) fresh or frozen blueberries

Wet ingredients

  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon almond extract (optional)
  • 1 large egg

Topping

  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar (reserved from above)
  • Cold butter, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Spray a 12-cup muffin tin with nonstick baking spray, including the flat top around the cups. (Line with cupcake liners if desired.)
  2. Whisk flour, 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons sugar, cornstarch, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon together in a large bowl. Add blueberries; toss to coat.
  3. Whisk milk, melted butter, vanilla, almond extract (if using), and egg together in a measuring cup. Pour over dry ingredients; fold gently until batter just forms with small lumps — stop slightly before it looks done.
  4. Divide batter among muffin cups; sprinkle each with 1/4 teaspoon of reserved sugar. Bake, rotating tin halfway through, until muffins are domed and light golden, 20–24 minutes. Cool in tin 5 minutes; remove and serve hot with cold butter.

    Soured milk

    • 2 cups whole milk

    Dry ingredients

    • 4 cups + 2 tablespoons (535 g) all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
    • 7 1/2 teaspoons (2 tablespoons + 1 1/2 teaspoons) aluminum-free baking powder, leveled
    • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
    • 2 tablespoons sugar
    • 2 teaspoons fine sea salt

    Butter

    • 12 tablespoons (1 1/2 sticks; 170 g) cold butter

    Steps

    1. Make the soured milk: Stir the vinegar into the whole milk. Let stand at least 5 minutes while you prepare the dough (it will look slightly curdled — that's fine).

    8. Pat the dough into a rough 3/4-inch-thick rectangle. Cut into 8 squares — no need to cut rounds or reroll scraps.
    9. Arrange the squares close together on the skillet (or parchment-lined cookie sheet) to help them rise tall.
    10. Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until the tops are deeply golden brown and the biscuits have risen tall.

    • 2 egg yolks (from 4 large eggs, separated and chilled)

    • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

    • 6 tablespoons cake flour
    • 1/4 cup milk, chilled

    Steps

      Serves 2

      Source: Adapted from The Woks of Life (thewoksoflife.com)

      Ingredients

      Eggs

      • 4 large eggs
      • 1/4 teaspoon salt
      • 1/4 teaspoon sesame oil
      • 1/8 teaspoon white pepper

      Sauce

      • 3 medium tomatoes (about 1 pound), cut into wedges
      • 2 scallions, white and green parts separated, sliced
      • 3 tablespoons neutral oil, divided
      • 1/2 teaspoon salt
      • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
      • 2 teaspoons ketchup
      • 1 1/2 teaspoons soy sauce

      Steps

      1. Beat eggs with 1/4 teaspoon salt, sesame oil, and white pepper until well combined.
      2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a wok or nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add eggs and scramble gently, folding rather than stirring, until just set but still slightly wet. Transfer to a plate.
      3. Add remaining 1 tablespoon oil to wok. Add scallion whites and stir-fry 30 seconds. Add tomatoes, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and sugar. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes break down and release their juices, about 3 minutes.
      4. Stir in ketchup and soy sauce.
      5. Return eggs and scallion greens to wok. Toss gently to combine. Serve immediately over rice.
