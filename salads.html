Butter Lettuce and Citrus Salad

Serves 2–3

"Supreming" citrus means cutting away the peel and pith, then slicing between the membranes to release clean segments.

Source: The Gourmandise School (The Pizza Class)

Ingredients

  • 1 head butter lettuce
  • 1 shallot
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 1 grapefruit or orange
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • ¼ cup olive oil

Steps

  1. Remove any wilted leaves from the butter lettuce. Tear into bite-sized pieces and place in a medium bowl.
  2. Mince the shallot and place in a small bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. Supreme (segment) your grapefruit right over the small bowl to catch any juices. Set the segmented citrus aside.
  4. Add lemon juice to the shallot bowl, then whisk in the olive oil. Dress the butter lettuce with the citronette. Plate and tuck the segmented citrus into and on top of the greens.

Charred Broccoli and Cauliflower Salad

6 to 8 | Total: 30 min | Active: 25 min

Great as a side at a barbecue with grilled seafood or meat, or to take to a potluck.

Source: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/ree-drummond/charred-broccoli-and-cauliflower-salad-19673188

Ingredients

Vegetables

  • 1 medium crown broccoli, broken into large florets
  • 1 medium cauliflower, broken into large florets
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

Dressing

  • 2/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/3 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • Pinch kosher salt
  • Pinch freshly ground black pepper

Salad

  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1/2 cup dried blueberries, plus extra for garnish
  • 1/4 cup pickled red onions, plus extra for garnish
  • 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds, plus extra for garnish

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Toss the broccoli and cauliflower with the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Grill, turning as needed, until tender and well charred, 8–10 minutes. Transfer to a large bowl.
  3. Make the dressing: whisk together the mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, dill, parsley, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Spread the dressing onto a serving platter, leaving a well in the middle.
  5. Add the arugula, blueberries, pickled red onions, and sunflower seeds to the bowl with the charred vegetables and toss to combine.
  6. Mound the vegetable mixture into the well. Garnish with extra blueberries, pickled red onions, and sunflower seeds. Serve.

Coleslaw Salad

Source: Family recipe card (Grandmother Brenda)

Ingredients

Dressing

  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 2 teaspoons salt

Salad

  • 1 package coleslaw mix
  • Dried cranberries, to taste
  • Slivered almonds, toasted, to taste

Steps

  1. Toast slivered almonds in a dry skillet over medium heat, stirring frequently, until golden and fragrant, about 3–4 minutes. Let cool.
  2. Whisk together all dressing ingredients until sugar dissolves.
  3. Combine coleslaw mix, dried cranberries, and toasted almonds in a large bowl.
  4. Pour dressing over salad and toss. Let sit at least 15 minutes before serving so the cabbage softens slightly.

Joan's on Third Butter Lettuce Salad (Copycat)

Serves 4

Original recipe uses French feta; family uses goat cheese in oil instead. Shallot is in the original but was skipped. Dressing recipe from CopyKat Recipes.

Source: Copycat recipe based on Joan's on Third, Los Angeles

Ingredients

Dressing

  • ¼ cup white wine vinegar (or champagne vinegar)
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ teaspoon lemon juice
  • Dried oregano, to taste
  • Dried basil, to taste
  • 9 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Salad

  • 1–2 large heads butter lettuce, washed, dried, and torn
  • ½ cup goat cheese, crumbled
  • ⅓ cup dried cranberries

Steps

  1. Make the dressing:
    • Whisk together the white wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, lemon juice, oregano, and basil in a small bowl.
    • Slowly drizzle in the olive oil while whisking constantly until emulsified.
    • Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  2. Place the torn butter lettuce in a large serving bowl.
  3. Sprinkle the crumbled goat cheese and dried cranberries evenly over the lettuce.
  4. Drizzle the vinaigrette lightly over the top just before serving and toss gently to combine.

Summer Salad

The dressing makes more than needed for one salad — leftovers keep refrigerated for 1–2 weeks.

Source: Family recipe card (Grandmother Brenda)

Ingredients

Dressing

  • 1 cup vinegar
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard (dry or prepared)
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Salad

  • 1 bag romaine lettuce
  • 5–6 mushrooms, diced
  • 1 container cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 to 1 mango, cubed
  • 1/2 avocado, cubed
  • Handful of salted cashews
  • Handful of sunflower seeds

Steps

  1. Whisk together all dressing ingredients. Refrigerate until ready to use.
  2. Tear romaine into a large bowl. Add mushrooms, cherry tomatoes, mango, and avocado.
  3. Top with cashews and sunflower seeds.
  4. Drizzle dressing over salad just before serving and toss.

American

Broccoli Salad

Serves 4

Source: New York Times

Ingredients

Broccoli

  • 1 1/2 to 2 pounds fresh broccoli (about 4 cups)
  • Salt to taste

Dressing

  • 1 small red onion, cut into 1/2-inch cubes (about 1 cup)
  • 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Cook broccoli in salted boiling water for 5 minutes until crisp-tender. Drain and place in a salad bowl.
  2. Sprinkle onion cubes over broccoli.
  3. Whisk mustard and lemon juice together, then beat in oil; season with salt and stir in parsley.
  4. Spoon dressing over broccoli. Serve hot, warm, or cold.

Crunchy Romaine Toss

Serves 10-12

Source: From Christy Ponder

Ingredients

Sweet & Sour Dressing

  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 3 teaspoons soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Toasted noodle mixture

  • 1 package ramen noodles, uncooked, broken up (discard flavor packet)
  • 1 cup walnuts, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Salad

  • 1 bunch broccoli, coarsely chopped
  • 1 head romaine lettuce, washed and broken into pieces
  • 4 green onions, chopped

Steps

  1. Whisk together all dressing ingredients until the sugar dissolves. Set aside.
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, melt butter with olive oil. Add broken ramen noodles and walnuts; cook, stirring, until golden, about 5 minutes. Spread on paper towels to cool.
  3. In a large bowl, combine broccoli, romaine, and green onions. Add the cooled noodle mixture and toss to combine.
  4. Pour dressing over the salad and toss to coat well. Serve immediately.

Chuck Wagon Barbecued Pasta Salad

Serves 10

Source: Printed clipping

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 4 oz wagon wheel pasta

Salad

  • 1 (15-oz) can garbanzo beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1/3 cup chopped green sweet pepper
  • 1/3 cup chopped red sweet pepper
  • 8 oz smoked turkey breast, cubed
  • 4 oz mozzarella, cubed
  • 4 oz cheddar, cubed
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced into rings
  • 3 tablespoons sliced pitted ripe olives

Dressing

  • 1/3 cup bottled barbecue sauce
  • 3 tablespoons Dijon-style mustard

To serve

  • 1 head green and/or red-tip leaf lettuce
  • 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts
  • Thin sweet pepper strips (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook pasta in boiling water until al dente; rinse and drain.
  2. Combine garbanzo beans, peppers, turkey, cheeses, onion, and olives in a large bowl. Whisk barbecue sauce and mustard together; add to turkey mixture. Add pasta and toss. Cover and refrigerate 2 to 24 hours.
  3. To serve: line a platter with lettuce leaves; shred remaining lettuce and mound in center. Spoon pasta mixture over lettuce. Sprinkle with pine nuts and garnish with pepper strips if desired.

Chinese

Asian Pasta Salad

Dressing must be made the night before.
The dressing uses a raw egg yolk — if preferred, substitute 1 tablespoon mayonnaise.

Source: Family recipe card

Ingredients

Noodles and protein

  • 1 pound cappellini (thin spaghetti), cooked and drained
  • 1 package firm tofu, cubed

Vegetables

  • 1 handful fresh snow peas
  • 1 can water chestnuts, sliced
  • 1 bunch scallions, chopped

Toppings

  • 1 small package sesame seeds, slightly toasted
  • 1 package pine nuts (pignoli)

Dressing (overnight)

  • 4 tablespoons dry sherry
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce (or teriyaki sauce)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • Basil and Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes to taste
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Combine all dressing ingredients except oil and hot pepper. Refrigerate overnight.
  2. Before serving, stir oil and Aleppo pepper (or red pepper flakes) into dressing.
  3. Toss cappellini with tofu, snow peas, water chestnuts, and scallions.
  4. Add dressing and toss. Top with toasted sesame seeds and pine nuts.

Holly's Spicy Noodle Salad with Peanut Dressing

Serves 10 as appetizer, 6 as main

Asparagus, broccoli, or snow peas all work well in place of each other.

Source: Pacific Flavors by Hugh Carpenter; shared by Holly Arledge

Ingredients

Noodles

  • 1/4 pound dried Chinese spaghetti noodles (or thin spaghetti)
  • 2 tablespoons safflower oil (to toss after cooking)

Vegetables

  • 1 cup shredded iceberg lettuce
  • 1 cup fresh bean sprouts
  • 1 cup thinly sliced mushrooms
  • 1 pound pencil-thin asparagus (or broccoli or snow peas)
  • 1 large red bell pepper, sliced or julienned

Dressing

  • 1/4 cup white sesame seeds
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock, heated to boiling
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons dark soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon dry sherry
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons Chinese chili sauce (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup minced green onions
  • 2 tablespoons finely minced ginger
  • 1 large garlic clove, minced

Steps

  1. Combine all dressing ingredients; mix well.
  2. Cook noodles in lightly salted boiling water about 4 minutes until firm. Drain; toss with safflower oil to prevent sticking.
  3. Combine noodles and vegetables, add dressing, and toss.
