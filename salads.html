Barbecue Bacon Wedge Salad with Grilled Corn

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Contains bacon.
For pickled red onions, see Quick Pickled Red Onions in the Preserves & Pickles section — prepare at least 1 hour ahead.

Source: Food Network / Katie Lee Biegel

Ingredients

Pickled red onions (1 hour ahead)

  • 1 batch Quick Pickled Red Onions (see Pickling section)

Blue cheese dressing

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons buttermilk
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon sugar
  • Dash hot sauce
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese

Barbecue bacon

  • 1/4 cup barbecue sauce
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 pound slab bacon, cut into lardons

Grilled corn

  • 1 ear corn, husked
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

To serve

  • 1 head iceberg lettuce, quartered into wedges
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare Quick Pickled Red Onions (see Pickling section) at least 1 hour ahead.
  2. Make blue cheese dressing: whisk together mayo, buttermilk, vinegar, sugar, and hot sauce; season with salt and pepper. Fold in blue cheese. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
  3. Make barbecue bacon: combine barbecue sauce, vinegar, and brown sugar in a skillet over medium heat. Add bacon and cook, stirring, until caramelized and sticky, 8–10 minutes. Set aside.
  4. Brush corn with olive oil. Grill or pan-sear over high heat until charred in spots. Cut kernels from cob.
  5. To assemble: place a lettuce wedge on each plate. Top with cherry tomatoes, corn kernels, barbecue bacon, pickled onions, and blue cheese dressing. Season with salt and pepper.

Boston Lettuce and Endives Salad

🔗 Link

Serves 6

Source: Ricardo Cuisine

Ingredients

Dressing

  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon whole-grain mustard
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Salad

  • 4 cups Boston lettuce leaves (about 1 small head), torn
  • 2 white endives, halved and separated into leaves
  • 2 red endives, halved and separated into leaves
  • 1 red apple, cored and thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup red grapes, halved
  • 1/2 cup roasted walnuts

Steps

  1. Whisk together mayonnaise, cider vinegar, mustard, maple syrup, and garlic. Season with salt and pepper. Can be made ahead.
  2. Combine lettuce, endives, apple, grapes, and walnuts in a large bowl.
  3. Just before serving, drizzle dressing over salad and toss well.

Butter Lettuce and Citrus Salad

🔗 Link

Serves 2–3

"Supreming" citrus means cutting away the peel and pith, then slicing between the membranes to release clean segments.

Source: The Gourmandise School (The Pizza Class)

Ingredients

  • 1 head butter lettuce
  • 1 shallot
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 1 grapefruit or orange
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • ¼ cup olive oil

Steps

  1. Remove any wilted leaves from the butter lettuce. Tear into bite-sized pieces and place in a medium bowl.
  2. Mince the shallot and place in a small bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. Supreme (segment) your grapefruit right over the small bowl to catch any juices. Set the segmented citrus aside.
  4. Add lemon juice to the shallot bowl, then whisk in the olive oil. Dress the butter lettuce with the citronette. Plate and tuck the segmented citrus into and on top of the greens.

★ Crunchy Romaine Toss

🔗 Link

Serves 10-12

Source: From Christy Ponder

Ingredients

Sweet & Sour Dressing

  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 3 teaspoons soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Toasted noodle mixture

  • 1 package ramen noodles, uncooked, broken up (discard flavor packet)
  • 1 cup walnuts, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Salad

  • 1 bunch broccoli, coarsely chopped
  • 1 head romaine lettuce, washed and broken into pieces
  • 4 green onions, chopped

Steps

  1. Whisk together all dressing ingredients until the sugar dissolves. Set aside.
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, melt butter with olive oil. Add broken ramen noodles and walnuts; cook, stirring, until golden, about 5 minutes. Spread on paper towels to cool.
  3. In a large bowl, combine broccoli, romaine, and green onions. Add the cooled noodle mixture and toss to combine.
  4. Pour dressing over the salad and toss to coat well. Serve immediately.

Joan's on Third Butter Lettuce Salad (Copycat)

🔗 Link

Serves 4

Original recipe uses French feta; family uses goat cheese in oil instead. Shallot is in the original but was skipped. Dressing recipe from CopyKat Recipes.

Source: Copycat recipe based on Joan's on Third, Los Angeles

Ingredients

Dressing

  • ¼ cup white wine vinegar (or champagne vinegar)
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 2–3 cloves garlic, minced

Dumpling Tomato Salad with Chili Crisp Vinaigrette

🔗 Link

Serves 4 | Total: 20 min

Use your favorite chili crisp—it is the dominant flavor and will greatly impact the final dish; brands vary in saltiness and spice, so season accordingly.
Salting works wonders for out-of-season tomatoes, so this salad is good year-round.

Source: Hetty Lui McKinnon / NYT Cooking

Ingredients

Tomato salad

  • 2 1/2 pounds ripe tomatoes (any variety), cut into 1- to 2-inch pieces, at room temperature
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn, divided
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt (Diamond Crystal brand preferred)
  • Black pepper

Chili crisp vinaigrette

  • 3 tablespoons chili crisp (or chili oil)
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce, or more to taste

Dumplings

  • 1 pound frozen potsticker dumplings (not thawed)
  • 1–2 tablespoons neutral oil (canola or vegetable)

To serve

  • 1–2 tablespoons store-bought crispy fried shallots (optional)

Steps

  1. Place tomatoes on a large serving platter or in a bowl. Add garlic, half the basil, salt, and a big pinch of black pepper. Toss and set aside.

Transfer to a plate; repeat with remaining dumplings.
  4. Place warm dumplings over the tomato salad and drizzle with vinaigrette. Toss gently. Top with crispy fried shallots (if using) and remaining basil. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Ingredients

Cucumbers

  • 5 Persian cucumbers, cut into 1/2-inch thick rounds

  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar

  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 2 teaspoons canola oil

  • 1/2 teaspoon chili garlic sauce
